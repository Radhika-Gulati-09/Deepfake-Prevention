RESEARCH INTEGRITY & HONEST DISCLOSURES

Understanding Video Adversarial Protection

Veil is an experimental research prototype exploring defensive visual perturbations. We believe in total transparency about both potential friction and operational limitations.

✓

What Veil Does

  • Applies Spatio-Temporal Phase Shifts: Generates continuous, low-frequency carrier waves that modulate high-frequency pixel gradients without causing perceptual frame-to-frame flicker.
  • Smooth Facial Saliency Blending: Targets facial landmark zones with Gaussian radial falloff, minimizing artifacts in static backgrounds while concentrating entropy where face-swapping encoders operate.
  • Preserves Original Audio: Synchronously streams and re-encodes your original audio track directly in the browser when supported.
  • Guarantees Zero Server Transmission: 100% client-side execution. Your video bytes and biometrics are never uploaded, logged, or inspected by any cloud server.
✕

What Veil Does Not Do

  • No Universal Immunity: It does not guarantee prevention of all deepfakes, face reenactment, or diffusion-based generative video manipulation.
  • Vulnerable to Heavy Platform Re-Encoding: Aggressive social platform compression (e.g. downscaling to 480p, H.264 high quantization, or blurring) acts as a low-pass filter that can wash out subtle perturbations.
  • No "Magic Green Pixel" AI Kryptonite: We reject misleading claims that shifting arbitrary green channels foils AI. Robust adversarial resistance requires multi-scale feature disruption.
  • Not a Deepfake Detection System: This tool modifies videos defensively; it does not classify or detect whether an existing video has already been altered.

How the Browser Processing Engine Works

01

Frame Extraction & Face Tracking

Decodes incoming video frames in browser memory. Detects the face centroid using browser FaceDetector or adaptive skin chrominance with an exponential moving average (EMA) filter to prevent bounding-box jitter.

02

Temporally Coherent Perturbation

Computes a continuous mathematical carrier wave $P(x,y,t)$. By anchoring spatial phase to time $t$, adjacent frames vary smoothly, completely eliminating high-frequency temporal flicker.

03

Saliency Feathering

Applies a smooth cosine-bell spatial mask centered at the face, focusing perturbation on facial keypoints while fading to zero across outer borders to prevent harsh rectangular edge seams.

04

Client-Side Stream Re-Encoding

Renders perturbed frames to an internal canvas pipeline, captures the video stream, mixes in the original audio via Web Audio API, and encodes the final downloadable file with MediaRecorder.

Absolute Client-Side Privacy Architecture

Veil has no tracking scripts, no telemetry, and no remote video backend. Your video is loaded strictly into your browser's private memory sandbox. When you click "Clear Session & Memory" or close this browser tab, all temporary object URLs and decoded frame buffers are instantly purged and garbage collected.
